# Proposal template

The `write-a-proposal` skill fills this. Anything it cannot fill from your files stays as `{{MISSING: what is needed}}`.
Prices come only from `core/pricing-and-limits.md`.

```text
# Proposal for {{company}}, {{date}}

## What you told us
{{two lines, their words, quoted from the call write-up}}

## What we will do
{{one line offer}}
1. {{first job, named from their business}}
2. {{second job}}

## How it runs
{{steps with rough timing, from core/business.md "How a job runs"}}

## Price
{{copied exactly from core/pricing-and-limits.md, with what is included}}

## Not included
{{from core/pricing-and-limits.md and anything they asked for that we do not do}}

## Next step
{{one proposed date or action}}

{{sign-off copied from communication/emails-i-sent.md}}
```
