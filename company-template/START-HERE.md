# Start here

This is your company folder. Your AI reads it before every job.

1. Open this folder in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or another agent.
2. Say **start**. It reads your website, asks 12 questions one at a time, and writes down only what you say. About 45 minutes. You can dictate.
3. It ends by running your first morning brief, so you see a real result the same hour.

Things worth knowing:

- `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` are copies of `READ-FIRST.md`. They are how your tool finds the rules. Keep the three identical.
- Paste today's emails, calendar and tasks into `inbox/`, or connect read-only tools later.
- Ask "what do you know about my company?" at any time. It should say what is filled in and point at the empty parts. If it invents facts, the rules file is not being read.
- For the deeper setup with real cases, follow the `install-the-ai-os` skill in `.claude/skills/`.
- To add a job of your own, say "teach a job".

Back up the folder with git or a private sync. Never push it to a public place: it holds your business.
