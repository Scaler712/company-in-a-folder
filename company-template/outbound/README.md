# Outbound

What the cold outreach jobs read and write.

- `outbound/ideal-prospect.md`: who is worth contacting, and who is not
- `outbound/do-not-contact.md`: people and companies never to contact. Checked before every list and every draft
- `outbound/prospects.csv`: the working list, one row per person
- `outbound/log.md`: what was sent by you, when, and what came back. In the Reply cell, start
  with the date the reply arrived, then a colon, then their words, like
  `2027-03-02: "Do you take an old wrap off?"`. The weekly review counts a reply in the week it
  arrived, whatever date the message was sent

The AI never sends outreach. It builds the list, writes the drafts and prepares a file for your own sending tool. You send. Contacting people you have no relationship with is covered by law in most places: check the rules for the countries you contact before the first message.
