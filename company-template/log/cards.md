# Cards

One block per card, in order. The next card number is the last number here plus one.

```text
C-<n> | <date, and the time if you know it> | job: <skill> | tool: <tool> | to: <who> | change: <before> -> <after>
answer: <"yes C-<n>" with time, "no", "changed: see C-<m>", or "no answer: held">
done: <what was read back from the tool, where, when, or "not done">
```
