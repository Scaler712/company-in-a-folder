# Proposal template

The `write-a-proposal` skill fills this. Anything it cannot fill from your files stays as
`{{MISSING: what is needed}}`. Prices come only from `core/pricing-and-limits.md`.

```text
# Proposal for {{company}}, {{date}}

## What you told us
{{two lines, their words, quoted from the call write-up}}

## What we will do
{{one line offer}}
1. {{first job, named from their business}}
2. {{second job}}

## How it runs
{{steps with rough timing, from core/business.md "How a job runs"}}

## Price
{{copied exactly from core/pricing-and-limits.md, with what is included}}

## Not included
{{from core/pricing-and-limits.md and anything they asked for that we do not do}}

## Next step
{{one proposed date or action}}

{{sign-off copied from communication/emails-i-sent.md}}
```

## Filled example, for shape only

```text
# Proposal for a dental practice, 2027-01-19

## What you told us
"The frosting is peeling at the corners and it looks grubby from the street."
"We want the waiting room to stop feeling like a corridor."

## What we will do
New cut vinyl graphics on the four street windows, plus a pavement sign for the side entrance.
1. Strip the old frosting and apply new cut vinyl, four windows
2. One A-frame pavement sign, printed both sides

## How it runs
Site measure in week 1. Proof within 2 working days. Application on one morning, shop closed
for 2 hours at most. Invoice on the day.

## Price
Vinyl window graphics, $240 per window, 4 windows: $960
A-frame pavement sign, printed both sides: $210
Total: $1,170. 50% deposit before we cut, balance within 14 days of install.

## Not included
Window cleaning or repair. Frosted film above 2 square metres. Replacement A-frame faces later
($90 each).

## Next step
We hold the Thursday morning slot in week 3 for you until Friday.

Thanks, Vera
```

A real won proposal, with the client removed, is in `won/2027-01-19-dental-practice-windows.md`.
