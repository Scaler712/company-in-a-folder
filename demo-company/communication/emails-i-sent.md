# Emails I sent

Real emails the owner wrote. Names, addresses and companies removed. Wording kept exactly.
Bots copy the length, greeting, punctuation and sign-off from here.

## 1. Reply to a new lead, van fleet

Hi, thanks for getting in touch. Three vans is a proper job and we would rather see them than
guess, so the first step is ten minutes in our yard with a tape measure. I have Tuesday or
Thursday morning free. Which suits? Thanks, Vera

## 2. Polite no, job under our minimum

Hi, thanks for asking. One small board on its own comes to less than our minimum order, so by
the time we have set up the cutter it costs you more than it should. The stationery shop in town
does single boards and will look after you. Sorry to send you elsewhere. Thanks, Vera

## 3. Polite no, rush job

Hi, I wish I could say yes. We do not take work inside 48 hours, because every rush job pushes
two booked jobs late and then I am writing two more apologies. The first date we can actually
hit is the 19th. If that still helps, say the word and I will hold the slot. Thanks, Vera

## 4. Proposal cover note

Hi, the proposal is attached, one page, no surprises in it. The price covers the measure, the
letters, the fixings and our crew on the day. It does not cover taking the old sign down, which
is $240 if you want us to do it. Have a read and tell me what you want changed. Thanks, Vera

## 5. Follow-up after no reply

Hi, I am not chasing, I am just tidying my list. Is the sign still something you want this
spring, or has it moved down the pile? Either answer is fine and no is a perfectly good one.
Thanks, Vera

## 6. Apology for a late job

Hi, we were late and that is on us. The banner missed the courier on Friday and I am not going
to dress it up. I am reprinting both at no charge and Marcus will drop them in himself on
Wednesday. If the date has passed and they are no use to you, say so and I will credit the lot.
Thanks, Vera

## 7. Proof chase, short

Hi, the proof has been sitting with you since Monday. Nothing moves until it is signed, and the
bay slot is yours until Thursday. One reply and we are away. Thanks, Vera

## 8. Thank-you to a repeat client

Hi, thanks for the kind words and thanks for the referral. Two of the three sheets are already
cut for your next windows. It is nice working with someone who signs a proof the same day.
Thanks, Vera
