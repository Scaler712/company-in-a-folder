# Ideal prospect

Who is worth a cold message. Stricter than `core/clients.md`: this is who we go looking for.

## Company

A trade firm running its own vans: plumbers, heating engineers, builders, roofers, glaziers,
groundworkers, landscapers. Between 5 and 30 vans, because under 5 the owner drives one of them
and there is no budget conversation, and over 30 there is a procurement process we cannot win in
one message. Within a 40 mile drive of the shop, so an install is a half day and not a crew day
(`core/clients.md`). The one sign they need us now: vans on the road with no livery, or livery
that does not match the newest vans.

## Person

- Feels the problem: the operations lead or fleet manager. They are the one who signs for a new
  van and then has to look at it sitting white on the yard.
- Signs the order: the owner, or the operations lead with the owner's budget. On a fleet of 9 it
  is usually the same person.

## Signs that it is a good moment

Things you can see from outside, with the page and the date written in the CSV:

- A public post or a local paper piece about new vans, a new depot, or a hiring push.
- A job advert for a driver, a fitter or an apprentice. More staff means more vans.
- Half the fleet in one livery and half plain, visible in photos on their own site.
- A rebrand: new logo on the site, old logo still on the vans.
- A new second location, so the vans now cross two towns where nobody knows them.
- An award or a contract win they published. They are feeling good about looking good.

## Never contact

- Political campaign offices and party groups, because political campaign work is a written no
  in `core/clients.md`.
- Other sign and print shops, because we would be pitching a competitor and they talk to each
  other at the trade fair.
- Anyone on `do-not-contact.md`, checked before a list is built and again before it is saved.
- Firms whose only work needs access above 6 metres, because we turn that work down anyway.
- Anyone who has asked us to stop, forever, no matter who asks for the list later.
- People at their home address or on a personal profile. Role and company only.
