# How we take and run a banner order

Written from what Jonas in the print room and Jana at the front desk actually do. Checked by
both on 2027-03-04. Changed on 2027-03-01 by decision D-2 in `decisions.md`: every banner order
now gets a named 48 hour print slot booked at order time, instead of "this week".

Nine steps, in order. If a step cannot be done, stop and tell Priya. Do not promise a date.

1. Write down what the client actually asked for, in their words: how many banners, what size,
   what the artwork is for, and the date they need them in their hands. The date they need them
   is not the date of their event. Ask for both.
2. Check the size against `core/pricing-and-limits.md`. A 3 by 1 metre hemmed banner with eyelets
   is $160 each. Any other size is quoted by Cleo, not by the front desk.
3. Check the date. We take no work inside 48 hours at any price (`core/clients.md`). If the date
   they need is less than 48 hours from the signed proof, say no plainly and give the first date
   we can actually hit.
4. Book the print slot now, in the shared shop calendar, as a named 48 hour window with the
   client's name on it. Not "this week". If the next free window is after their date, go back to
   step 3.
5. Take payment. A banner order of $800 or less is paid in full on order. Over $800 takes a 50%
   deposit. Hedda handles the card or transfer at the counter.
6. Send the artwork to Omar for a proof. Two proofs are included, a third is $85 an hour of
   design time. The print queue does not start before the client has signed the proof in writing.
7. Print on the booked slot, hem and fit eyelets the same day. Jonas writes the finished time in
   the print room log, which is how we prove the priority in `core/priorities.md`.
8. Tell the client it is ready the moment it is off the machine, and say whether it is collection
   or courier. Courier is not included in the price, so it is agreed before it is booked.
9. Write the collection or courier date in the print room log next to the finished time. A banner
   that leaves late gets a line saying why, the same day, not a week later.

## What goes wrong

- The three late banners in February all missed step 4. They were taken as "this week" and the
  print room filled up around them (print room log, 2027-02-28).
- One of them went out after the event it was for. That is the rowing club complaint sitting in
  `inbox/email.md`. The owner answers that one, not a bot.
