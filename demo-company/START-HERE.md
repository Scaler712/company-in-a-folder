# Start here (this is the demo)

This folder is a **demo company**. Everything in it is made up: Tidewater Sign & Print, a
12-person sign and print shop. No real business, person, price or email is in here.

Nothing is left blank, so you can try the kit without filling anything in first.

Open `TRY-THIS.md` and paste the five prompts in order. It takes about two minutes.

When you want your own folder, do not edit this one. Copy the empty template instead:

```bash
python3 scripts/new_company.py ~/my-company
```
