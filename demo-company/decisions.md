Copy this block for each entry. Newest at the bottom.

```text
## D-<n> | <date> | proposed by: <job or person>
proposal: <what changes, in one line>
why: <the evidence, with sources>
expected result: <what should be different, and how we will see it>
check on: <date + 7 days>
owner answer: <yes / no / changed, with date>
actual (filled on the check date): <what happened, with sources>
keep, change or drop: <decision>
```
