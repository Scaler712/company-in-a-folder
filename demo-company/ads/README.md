# Paid ads

What the ads jobs read and write.

- `ads/numbers.md`: what a customer is worth and what you can pay to get one. Every ads job reads it first
- `ads/tests.md`: what is being tested, what changed, and what happened

With no tool connected, save the export from your ad platform in this folder with the dates in the file name, for example `ads/export-YYYY-MM-DD-to-MM-DD.csv`. The jobs read the newest one and say what an export cannot show.

The ads jobs read your ad accounts through a connected tool. See `tools/autoady.md` in the kit for the one they are written for. Reading is free of risk. Anything that spends money, turns an ad on or off, or changes a budget goes through an approval card.
