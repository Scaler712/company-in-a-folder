# Business

What we do, written so a new senior hire could explain it on day one.

Tidewater Sign & Print. 12 people. One shop with a print room, a wrap bay and two install vans.
We serve local businesses in Fairhaven and the two towns along the bay. Founded 2014 by the
owner, Vera, who still signs every quote over $2,000.

## What we sell

- The 2 or 3 services that make the money:
  1. Shop front signs: flat cut acrylic or aluminium letters, surveyed, made and installed.
  2. Vehicle graphics: full van wraps and partial wraps for trade fleets.
  3. Printed banners, window graphics and A-frame pavement signs for events and promotions.
- What we do not do, even if asked: neon, illuminated box signs with wiring (we subcontract
  nothing and we are not electricians), anything needing a lift above 6 metres, screen printed
  clothing, and rush work inside 48 hours. See "Who we turn down" in `core/clients.md`.

## How a job runs

From first contact to final invoice, in steps:

1. First contact: the shop email, the site form, or someone walking in. Walk-ins are written up
   the same day by the front desk.
2. First call or site visit: 20 minutes on the phone, then a site measure for anything fixed to
   a building or a vehicle. We never quote a wrap from photos.
3. Quote or proposal: one page, priced only from `core/pricing-and-limits.md`. Target is 2
   working days from the measure.
4. Delivery: artwork proof, client sign-off in writing, print or cut, then install. Banners are
   print and collect or courier. Signs and wraps are installed by our crew.
5. Invoice and follow-up: deposit on order, balance invoiced on the install day, then a check-in
   two weeks later.

## Tools we use

- Email: the shop inbox, hosted mail, shared by the front desk and the owner.
- Calendar and booking: the shared shop calendar. No public booking link. Jana at the front desk
  books calls by replying with two time slots.
- CRM or client list: a spreadsheet, one row per client, kept by the shop manager.
- Where files live: the shared drive, one folder per job, named year then client.
- Print queue and job tickets: RipDesk.
- Bookkeeping and invoices: Ledgerly.

## How we sound

- Greeting and sign-off we use: "Hi <first name>," and "Thanks, Vera". No "Dear".
- Words we never use: "reach out", "solutions", "touch base", "we'd love to" (say "happy to"),
  "guarantee".
- Real samples live in `communication/emails-i-sent.md`.
