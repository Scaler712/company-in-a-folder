# Pricing and limits

Bots quote this file exactly or not at all. A price that is not written here does not exist.
Prices are in US dollars and exclude tax. Last reviewed 2027-02-01.

## Prices

| Service | Price | What is included | What is not included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shop front sign, flat cut letters, up to 2 metres wide | $1,850 installed | Site measure, artwork, cutting, fixings, install by our crew | Removal of an old sign, making good the wall, permits, anything over 2 metres |
| Shop front sign, over 2 metres wide | Quoted per job after the measure | Site measure, written quote within 2 working days | Anything needing a lift above 6 metres (we do not take it) |
| Vinyl window graphics | $240 per window | Artwork, cut vinyl, application on site | Window cleaning or repair, frosted film above 2 square metres |
| Full van wrap | $3,400 per van | Measure, design, print, wrap, 2 day bay slot | Panel repair, de-badging, removal of an old wrap ($450 per van) |
| Partial van wrap, doors and rear | $1,250 per van | Design, print, application | Bonnet and roof panels |
| Event banner, 3 by 1 metre, hemmed with eyelets | $160 each | Print, hem, eyelets, collection from the shop | Rigging, courier, same week delivery |
| A-frame pavement sign, printed both sides | $210 each | Frame, both printed faces | Replacement faces later ($90 each) |
| Design time beyond the first hour | $85 per hour | Artwork changes after the second proof | Logo design from scratch (we do not do it) |
| Old sign removal and disposal | $240 per site | Removal, disposal, basic clean of the fixing points | Plaster or brick repair |

- Minimum order: $150. Below that we say no, see `core/clients.md`.
- Rush surcharge: none, because we do not take work inside 48 hours at any price.

## Payment terms

- Orders over $800: 50% deposit before we print or cut, balance within 14 days of install.
- Orders of $800 or less: paid in full on order.
- Repeat clients with three paid invoices may pay the full amount within 14 days of install.
- Card or transfer, handled at the counter by the bookkeeper. No account details live in this
  folder, ever.
- Late balance: we stop the next job for that client until it clears. The owner makes the call.

## Discounts

- Allowed: none as standard. Fleet work of 4 vans or more and repeat clients may get something,
  but only the owner decides, case by case, in writing.
- Bots never offer, hint at, or agree to a discount. They pass the request to the owner.

## Where price is never mentioned

- First replies to a new lead
- Public posts and pages
- Any wrap quote before we have seen the vehicle in person
- Any sign over 2 metres before the site measure

## Limits

- Promises we never make: a delivery date inside 48 hours, a print date before the proof is
  signed, colour matching to a printed sample we have not held in our hands, how long a wrap
  lasts on a vehicle we have not inspected.
- Work we never take on: see "Who we turn down" in `core/clients.md`.
- The most we take on at once: 2 wrap bay slots and 3 install days per week. The print room runs
  one banner run a day, usually before noon.
