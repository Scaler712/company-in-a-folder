# People

Who is who, so a bot knows who to ask and who signs off.
Roles matter more than names. 12 people, 9 of them listed here by role.

| Name or role | What they handle | What they approve | How to reach them inside the company |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vera, owner | Quotes over $2,000, all money and terms, anything a client is unhappy about | Every card. Money, terms, discounts, hiring. | In the shop, or the owner line in the shared inbox |
| Priya, shop manager | The week's schedule, the client spreadsheet, who goes to which install | Job order changes inside the week | Shop manager chat in the shared inbox |
| Omar, design lead | Artwork, proofs, print files, the two proof limit | Proofs going to a client once the owner has seen the price | Design queue in RipDesk |
| Jana, front desk | Walk-ins, the shared inbox, booking calls, writing up what a client said | Nothing. She passes everything on. | Front desk, shared inbox |
| Cleo, estimator | Site measures, quote sheets, the 2 working day quote target | Nothing. Quotes go to the owner. | Quote log on the shared drive |
| Teo, wrap installer | The wrap bay, vehicle prep, wrap removal | Bay slot order on the day | Wrap bay |
| Marcus, install crew lead | Sign installs, ladders and access, making good | Calling off an install for weather or access | Install van radio |
| Jonas, print operator | Large format printing, hemming and eyelets, the daily banner run | Nothing. The queue is Priya's. | Print room |
| Hedda, bookkeeper (part time, Wednesdays) | Invoices, deposits, chasing balances in Ledgerly | Nothing. Write-offs are the owner's. | Wednesdays in the shop |

The other three are a second install hand, an apprentice in the print room, and a weekend
Saturday counter person. None of them appear in bot work.

## Who may say "yes C-<n>"

- The owner, Vera.
- Nobody else. Priya may ask for a card to be shown again, but she cannot approve it.
