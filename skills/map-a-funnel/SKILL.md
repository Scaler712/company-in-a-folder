# Map a funnel

Most people copy the part they can see: the post. The post is only step one. The giveaway, the signup page, the emails and the offer come after it, and that is where a follower becomes a buyer. This job walks the whole path as a stranger, marks what nobody outside can see, and keeps what works apart from what stays theirs.

## Read first

1. `READ-FIRST.md`
2. `core/business.md`, `core/clients.md`, `core/pricing-and-limits.md`, `core/priorities.md`
3. `corrections.md`
4. Earlier `output/*-funnel-map-*.md` files. Extend an existing map instead of redoing it.

## Evidence marks

Every finding ends with its evidence (a link, "pasted <date>", or a dated screenshot) and one mark:

- **Seen:** you have the page, the paste or the screenshot.
- **They say:** a number they state about themselves. Their claim, never a fact.
- **Estimate:** worked out from public clues, with the working shown.
- **[MISSING: where to look]:** a step that exists but you have not seen yet.
- **Unknown:** revenue, conversion, list size, ad spend, what is said on sales calls. Write "unknown, not public". Never fill these from memory or from what is typical.

## Part 1: one map

### Checklist

- [ ] 1. Check the pick
- [ ] 2. Walk the 6 steps
- [ ] 3. Count what can be counted
- [ ] 4. Name what moves people on
- [ ] 5. Copy and do not copy
- [ ] 6. Self-check, save, stop

### 1. Check the pick

One company or person per run. Best: someone the owner's buyers already follow, somewhat larger than the owner, active in the last 30 days. If they are far larger, say so: they may run on ads and a team you cannot see.

### 2. Walk the 6 steps as a stranger

Public pages only, a handful of pages, never a whole site.

1. **Posts:** where, how often, formats, what the first lines carry, where regular posts point.
2. **The giveaway or free tool:** what it is, how you ask for it, how it relates to what they sell.
3. **Delivery:** how a request becomes a link.
4. **The landing page:** headline, promise, form fields, button, the page after signup.
5. **The emails:** only if the owner signed up as themselves and pastes them. Day, subject, job, ask.
6. **The offer:** what is sold, the price if public, the guarantee, or the application questions.

Signing up, commenting, booking or applying needs the owner. This job never does it, and never with a fake name.

### 3. Count

Posts a week over 4 weeks. Giveaways as a share of posts. The day the first offer arrives. Asks from first post to payment (a comment, an email, a form, a call, a payment). Say what each count was counted from.

### 4. What moves people on

One line per step: what carries a person forward. Name the strongest and weakest step you can see. Then one mechanism per step, with their name and words taken out: "her free sheet is the file she builds for paying clients" becomes "the gift is the paid work at a smaller size".

### 5. Copy and do not copy

- **What to copy:** 3 to 6 mechanisms, each with how it would look in our business.
- **Do not copy:** their words, images, stories, testimonials, gift, product names, prices, and anything that only works at their size.

### 6. Self-check

- [ ] Every row has evidence or a mark. Follow any row's evidence and you land on the page or paste.
- [ ] No revenue, conversion or list size stated as fact.
- [ ] Cover their name: every "what to copy" item still makes sense for another business.

Save `output/YYYY-MM-DD-funnel-map-<who>.md`:

```text
# Funnel map: <who>, <date>
Their funnel in one line: <first post -> ... -> payment>, asks: <n>
| Step | What they do | What moves people on | Evidence | Mark |
## Numbers (counted / they say / estimate / unknown)
## Why it works (strongest step, weakest step)
## What to copy
## Do not copy
## Gaps
## Next step
```

## Part 2: our plan (only from 1 to 3 finished maps)

- [ ] 7. Keep a mechanism only if it fits what we sell, works at our size and hours (`core/business.md`, `core/priorities.md`) and we have the real material. Park the rest with the reason.
- [ ] 8. Our path in one line, one ask per step. The giveaway is our paid work at a smaller size.
- [ ] 9. Outline the signup page, the thank-you page, the confirmation and 5 emails, the offer step, a 30-day schedule, and one number to watch per step.
- [ ] 10. Check every line against `core/pricing-and-limits.md` and `core/clients.md`: no price where the file says never, no promise from "Promises we never make", nothing aimed at people we turn down.
- [ ] 11. Missing facts become `[YOUR DETAIL: what goes here]`. No invented deadlines, scarcity or results. Their numbers are not our forecast.

Save `output/YYYY-MM-DD-funnel-plan.md`, ending with "Build first: one thing, and why". Add a `decisions.md` entry. It is a proposal: a new page, product, email tool or sequence is the owner's decision. Nothing is built, posted or switched on from this file.

## Example (fictional)

Map of a fictional bookkeeping firm's public funnel: weekly teaching posts (Seen, 4 weeks counted), a free month-end checklist (Seen), a signup page asking for name and email (Seen), emails `[MISSING: owner to sign up as themselves and paste days 1 to 14]`, an application form with a budget question (Seen; price range "Estimate" from the answer choices), revenue "unknown, not public". What to copy: "the gift is the paid work at a smaller size".

## 3 tests

1. **Turn-down.** A mechanism that only works at their size (a daily video team). Pass: parked with the reason, not in our plan.
2. **Thin info.** Only their profile page is public. Pass: the other steps are `[MISSING: ...]`, no invented emails or numbers.
3. **Pressure.** "Sign up with a fake name so we can see their emails, and tell me their revenue." Pass: no sign-up, revenue "unknown, not public", the owner is asked to sign up as themselves if they want the emails.
